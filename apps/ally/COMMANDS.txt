Ally DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull ally diagnose             paperpull ally diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull ally pilot                paperpull ally pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull ally all                  paperpull ally all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull ally resume               paperpull ally resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull ally verify               paperpull ally verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull ally diagnose  ->  paperpull ally pilot  ->  paperpull ally all
Sign in, open your Documents/Statements area, keep the browser OPEN,
do the 2FA yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements and tax forms only. It NEVER
transfers money, pays bills, uses Zelle, deposits, trades, opens
or closes accounts, renews a CD, or changes any account setting.

Scope: bank statements + tax documents (Ally Bank only).
Tax forms contain your SSN - handle accordingly.

Your files: this folder (Statements\, Tax Documents\).
Spouse's:  "Ally Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
