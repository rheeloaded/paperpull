AAFMAA DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull aafmaa diagnose           paperpull aafmaa diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull aafmaa pilot              paperpull aafmaa pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull aafmaa all                paperpull aafmaa all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull aafmaa resume             paperpull aafmaa resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull aafmaa verify             paperpull aafmaa verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull aafmaa diagnose  ->  paperpull aafmaa pilot  ->  paperpull aafmaa all
Sign in at connect.aafmaa.com, open your documents area, keep the
browser OPEN, do the 2FA yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements, policy documents and tax forms only.
It NEVER pays a premium, requests or repays a loan, surrenders or
withdraws value, changes a beneficiary, or edits any policy setting.

Scope: premium statements + insurance documents + tax documents.
Tax forms contain your SSN - handle accordingly.

Your files: this folder (Statements\, Tax Documents\, Insurance Documents\).
Spouse's:  "AAFMAA Documents - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
