Chase DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull chase diagnose            paperpull chase diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull chase pilot               paperpull chase pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull chase all                 paperpull chase all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull chase resume              paperpull chase resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull chase verify              paperpull chase verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull chase diagnose  ->  paperpull chase pilot  ->  paperpull chase all
Sign in, open your Documents/Statements area, keep the browser OPEN,
do the 2FA yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements only. It NEVER
transfers money, pays bills, uses Zelle, deposits, trades, opens
or closes accounts, renews a CD, or changes any account setting.

Scope: credit-card statements only (no tax or year-end docs).

Your files: this folder (Statements\).
Spouse's:  "Chase Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
