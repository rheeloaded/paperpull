READ-ONLY: downloads monthly statements (PDF and CSV), the yearly
transactions CSV and tax forms only. It NEVER buys, sells, sends,
receives, deposits, withdraws, opens a loan, or changes any setting.

Scope: monthly account statements, each as a PDF with its CSV beside
it, the annual transactions CSV, and tax forms (1099s).
Tax forms contain your SSN - handle accordingly.

Your files: this folder (Statements\, Tax Documents\).
Spouse's:  "Strike Statements - spouse".
