Discover Bank DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull discoverbank diagnose     paperpull discoverbank diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull discoverbank pilot        paperpull discoverbank pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull discoverbank all          paperpull discoverbank all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull discoverbank resume       paperpull discoverbank resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull discoverbank verify       paperpull discoverbank verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull discoverbank diagnose  ->  paperpull discoverbank pilot  ->  paperpull discoverbank all
Sign in, open your Documents/Statements area, keep the browser OPEN,
do the 2FA yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements only. It NEVER transfers funds,
sends wires or Zelle, pays bills, opens/closes accounts, or changes any setting.

Scope: Discover Bank Checking and Savings statements.
Credit cards (migrated to Capital One) and loans are NOT covered.

STATUS: verify against the live site with paperpull discoverbank diagnose.
See README.md.

Your files: this folder (Statements\).
Spouse's:  "Discover Bank Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
