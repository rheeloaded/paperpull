USAA DOCUMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull usaa diagnose             paperpull usaa diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull usaa pilot                paperpull usaa pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull usaa all                  paperpull usaa all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull usaa resume               paperpull usaa resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull usaa verify               paperpull usaa verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull usaa diagnose  ->  paperpull usaa pilot  ->  paperpull usaa all
Sign in, open your Documents/Statements area, keep the browser OPEN,
do the 2FA yourself.

READ-ONLY: downloads statements, tax forms, and insurance documents
only. It NEVER transfers money, pays bills, uses Zelle, deposits,
trades, files claims, or changes any account/policy setting.

Scope: bank statements + tax documents + insurance documents.
Tax forms contain your SSN - handle accordingly.

Your files: this folder (Statements\, Tax Documents\, Insurance Documents\).
Spouse's:  "USAA Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
