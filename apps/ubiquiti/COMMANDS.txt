Ubiquiti Store receipt commands from the PaperPull checkout

paperpull ubiquiti login      Open the sign-in window
paperpull ubiquiti discover   List the account's paid orders
paperpull ubiquiti pilot      Save a sample of the newest receipts
paperpull ubiquiti all        Save every order's receipt
paperpull ubiquiti resume     Continue an interrupted download
paperpull ubiquiti verify     Recheck saved PDFs
