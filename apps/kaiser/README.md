# Kaiser Permanente Bills

In PaperPull, open **Kaiser Permanente Bills**, click **Open sign-in window**, and sign in
in the dedicated browser. Open the list you want to archive.

- For bills, notices, and other documents in **My Documents**, sign in and
  leave that page open. PaperPull reads every results page.
- For medical bills, choose **Pay Medical Bills**, open **View billing and
  payment details** for the billing account, and select **Statements**.

Keep one document list open at a time. Choose **Discover** to list available documents
or **Try the newest few first** to save one before choosing **Get new documents**.

PaperPull renders premium bills from Kaiser's bill viewer and saves medical
statements as the original PDFs. It verifies the medical PDF against the
selected statement date before saving. Bills go in `Bills` and other
documents go in `Other Documents`.

If Kaiser signs you out, use **Open sign-in window** to sign back in. Leave
the same document list open for **Continue**. PaperPull waits for **My
Documents** to load and picks up the pending downloads within the original
run's limits. For medical statements, reopen the same billing account's
**Statements** view. If PaperPull cannot match a billing statement to the
earlier list, verify the account in the browser and choose **Get new
documents**.
