# Kaiser Permanente Bills

In PaperPull, open **Kaiser Permanente Bills**, click **Open sign-in window**, and sign in
in the dedicated browser. Open the list you want to archive.

- For premium bills, choose **View past bills** and leave **My Documents** open.
- For medical bills, choose **Pay Medical Bills**, open **View billing and
  payment details** for the billing account, and select **Statements**.

Keep one bill list open at a time. Choose **Discover** to list available bills
or **Try the newest few first** to save one before choosing **Get new documents**.

PaperPull renders premium bills from Kaiser's bill viewer and saves medical
statements as the original PDFs. It verifies the medical PDF against the
selected statement date before saving. Both types go in the connector's
`Bills` folder. Kaiser's payment history has its own transaction entries.
