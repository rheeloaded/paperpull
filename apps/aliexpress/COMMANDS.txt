AliExpress receipt and seller invoice commands from the PaperPull checkout

paperpull aliexpress login      Open the sign-in window
paperpull aliexpress discover   List every order's receipt and seller invoices
paperpull aliexpress pilot      Save a sample of the newest
paperpull aliexpress all        Save every order's receipt and invoices
paperpull aliexpress resume     Continue an interrupted download
paperpull aliexpress verify     Recheck saved PDFs
