Cloudflare invoice commands from the PaperPull checkout

paperpull cloudflare login      Open the sign-in window
paperpull cloudflare discover   List the selected account's invoices
paperpull cloudflare pilot      Save a sample of the newest invoices
paperpull cloudflare all        Save the invoice history
paperpull cloudflare resume     Continue an interrupted download
paperpull cloudflare verify     Recheck saved PDFs
